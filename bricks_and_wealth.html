    Two meanings of typical · ILOSTAT urban vs. rural pay

    Does the place with higher average pay also have higher median pay?

    Each point locates one survey year (2016–2025) by its urban-minus-rural mean pay gap (horizontal) and median pay gap (vertical) on the same EGP/month scale. Shaded quadrants mark sign reversals where mean and median crown different places.

    Survey means and medians summarize location, not the full income distribution. A sign reversal (as in 2025) indicates a heavier upper tail in urban wages — it does not measure what share of rural workers out-earn urban workers.
